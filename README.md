# Metro AI Studio

Every AI tool the Metro claims team needs, in one simple studio, for the adjusters, claims reps, sales team and office staff of [Metro Public Adjustment, Inc.®](https://metropa.com).

**Live preview:** https://bizconnectcoalition.github.io/metropa-ai-studio/

Write plain-English homeowner explainers and carrier emails, make handouts, safety tips and recruiting graphics, build rep-training slide decks, translate homeowner notices, record training voiceovers and talking-avatar clips, and put together outreach and brand kits, all in plain words, with AI Steve (a cartoon guide modeled on Metro's founder) to help.

Built on the BizConnect AI Studio core (a white-label AI studio). Developers and AI agents: see [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).

*Preview. Sign-in uses a private access passcode. AI drafts need human and compliance review before use.*
